Side panel

On job sites JobScript supports, a small “JobScript” button sits at the bottom right of application pages. It opens the side panel, which can do everything the toolbar popup does.

The panel only lists and fills fields when you press Fill. Showing it doesn’t fill anything.

Saving answers

When you answer a question JobScript couldn’t, it offers to save the answer for next time: to your custom answers above, and for that site’s exact field. On multi-step forms, the side panel can save a whole step at once.

Uploads, checkboxes, demographic, reference and sensitive questions are never saved. Dates are saved as the exact date unless you pick a rule like “2 weeks from today”.

Sites

Answers saved for one site’s exact fields, and the steps of sites you taught JobScript with “Learn this site’s steps” in the popup.

AI answers (optional)

For questions JobScript can’t fill from your profile, Claude can suggest answers. Suggestions are never typed in until you accept them, and you pay Anthropic directly with your own API key.

What turning this on sends to Anthropic

When you click Fill, for each question JobScript couldn’t answer, it sends Anthropic:

  • the question and its answer options
  • your profile: name, city, links, work authorization, skills, work history, education and saved answers
  • your resume text, with email, phone and street address removed
  • the job title and description from the page

It never sends your email, phone, street address, ZIP, self-ID (EEO) answers, or questions about consent, signatures, SSN or date of birth. Anthropic’s API terms apply.

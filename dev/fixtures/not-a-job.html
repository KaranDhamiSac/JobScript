How to write a resume that gets read

Recruiters skim. Put your strongest work first, and match the words a posting uses where they're true of you.

Keep it to one page

Unless you have ten years of experience, one page is enough. Cut anything older than a decade.

Quantify

Numbers make results concrete: "cut reporting time by 40%" beats "improved reporting".
